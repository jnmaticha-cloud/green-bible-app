OMORANDIA.
6.
Mbore obobe narorie aiga inse y’erioba boritoerete Mwanyabaanto: 
Nyasae oeire omonto obotenenku, ne chinibo, na ogosikwa, na omonto oyio taremeiri gento kende ase ebio bionsi aganetie, korende Nyasae tamoeti okobua gw’okwegokia ase ebinto ebio; omonto omoao nigo akobiira bwegokia nabirobio. Ayio naro mbosa, naende nogwechanda okobe. 
Onye omonto oiboire abana rigana erimo, na abe moyo ase engaki y’emiaka emenge, ee, nonya amatuko ’obogima bwaye abeire amange, korende tegogetie ase ebinto ebiya bi’obogima obwo, na tatindekwa ase amasikani, nigo ngoteeba ng’a omwana okwerete enda ya ng’ina nigo are buya kobua omonto oyio. 
Omwana oyio okwete nigo achete egento bosa, naende nigo akogenda gochia ase omosunte, na omosunte oyio otuba erieta riaye. 
Omwana oria taroche erioba, gose tamanyete gento kende, korende nonya naboigo nigo akonyora omotimoko kobua omonto oria, 
nonya omonto oria ogochia komenya emiaka chilifu ibere, korende tanyora omogooko ase obogima bwaye. Inee! Abwo bonsi babere tibakogenda aase amo? 
Emeremo yonsi omonto akwerosia gokora nokonyora egento akoria, korende nonya naboigo tari koigota. 
Mbuya ki omonto omong’aini abwate kobua oyore omoriri? Gose ninki omonto omotaka akonyora, oria omanyete buna akwerenda ase abare moyo? 
Eki amaiso aroche nigo kere buya kobua eki okogania ng’a okenyore. Ayio boigo mbosa, na okominyokia aya are buna embeo bosa. 
Gento kende gionsi kereo nigo kiarogetwe erieta korwa kare, naende nigo yamanyekanete eki omonto are gocha koba. Mwanyabaanto takonyara koamererania noria ore ne chinguru komobua. 
Ogokwana okonge nigo gokorenta amang’ana amange are bosa. Inee! Ayio mbuya ki akorenta ase Mwanyabaanto? 
Ning’o omanyete egento kere ekiya ase omonto, ekero amenyete amatuko amake ase obogima bore bosa, agoeta buna omorengari? Ning’o oranyare goteebia omonto amang’ana arabe korwa magega yaye aiga inse y’erioba?
