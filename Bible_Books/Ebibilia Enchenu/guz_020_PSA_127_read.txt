ZABURI.
127.
(Ogoteera gwa Sulemani) Onye Omonene takoagaacha enyomba, abwo bakoyeagaacha nigo bagokora emeremo ya bosa. Onye Omonene takorenda omochie omonto oyio okoyorenda nigo akorenda bosa. 
Nigo ere bosa ase more kobooka mambia chuni na gokora emeremo emekong’u ase okonyora endagera, naende mwanyorwa gochia korara. Korende Omonene nigo agwechaandera abanto baye anchete, nonya nekero baraire chitoro. 
Rora, abana nigo bare omwando okorwa ase Omonene, ekiagera oroiboro rokorwa ase enda nigo rore ekeegwa. 
Abana omonto akonyora kare ne’chinguru chi’obosae bwaye, nigo banga buna chinsara chire koboko kw’omorwani etuoni. 
Nasesenirie omonto oyo oichoretie esaaro yaye ne chinsara echio. Oyio takobugwa ekero arabe okogamberana nababisa baye agwo ase ekegambero gia’etureti.
