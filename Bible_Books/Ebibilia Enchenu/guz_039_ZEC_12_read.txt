ZAKARIA.
12.
Eri n’ering’ana rikong’u ri’Omonene igoro y’Abaisraeli: Aya naro Omonene agoteeba, oyio oramborete igoro chionsi na akabeeka oboroso bw’ense, oyio otongete omoika ime ase omonto: 
“Rora, inche nigo inde ang’e gokora Yerusalemu obe buna egekombe kere nekenyugwa kegotindia ase abanto bonsi bamenyete ase chinsemo chiaye chionsi, boigo nkebanyore Abayuda ekero Yerusalemu orache koboerwa. 
Ekero engaki eyio eraike, ninkore Yerusalemu obe buna rigena ririto ase abanto bonsi; bonsi abarateeme korietweka mbateke chingoma abanyene. Abanto b’ebisaku bionsi bi’ense nabasangererekane amo bayoumokere. 
Ase engaki eyio nindutere kera ebarasi omoondoko, na omonto oyeriinete abe ebarimo. Korende nimbe ndigereretie na korenda Abayuda, ekero ndaukorie chibarasi chi’abaanto b’ebisaku.” Nabo Omonene atebiire. 
Erio abagaambi b’egesaku ki’Abayuda mbetebie ase chinkoro chiabo ime: “Abanto bamenyete Yerusalemu nigo babwate chinguru goetera ase Omonene bw’Emeganda, Nyasae obo. 
“Ase engaki eria ninkore abagaambi b’egesaku ki’Abayuda babe buna orogio rore nomorero ogosamba chinko, naende buna ekieri ki’omorero ogosamba emesaima y’amasakwe. Barabwo mbasambe abanto bonsi bamenyete ase chinsemo chiabo chionsi, basirie aande onsi, ensemo ya borio na eya bomosi, korende Yerusalemu nogenderere kobeera ase ore bono. 
“Inche Omonene nintoorie obomenyo bw’Abayuda ritang’ani, erinde abanto b’enyomba ya Daudi, na aba Yerusalemu, tibaabaisa gwetogia ng’a mbare nobonene igoro y’Abayuda. 
Ase engaki eyio Omonene nakengere abanto bamenyete Yerusalemu, erinde oyore omworo mono ase egati yabo abe buna Daudi, na abanto b’enyomba ya Daudi babe buna Nyasae, ee, buna omomalaika bw’Omonene oyo obatang’aine. 
Ase engaki eyio ninganie gosiria abanto b’ebisaku bionsi barache korwania Yerusalemu. 
“Ning’umorere abanto b’enyomba ya Daudi, na aba Yerusalemu, omoika bw’amaabera na ogosaba. Ekero barandigererie mbarore oyo babetete babe bakondeera, buna omonto okoreera omwana oye omomoima; mbandeere bare nobororo buna obw’omonto okoreera omwana oye omotangi. 
Ase engaki eyio, ebirero ebirabe ime ya Yerusalemu mbibe ebinene buna ebio biabeete igoro ya Hadadi‐Rimoni agwo ase ense omweya ya Megido. 
Abanto b’ense eye mbeumie, kera eamate ase ensemo yaye; eamate y’enyomba ya Daudi ensemo yaye, na abakungu babo ensemo yabo; eamate y’enyomba ya Nathani ensemo yaye, na abakungu babo ensemo yabo; 
eamate y’enyomba ya Lawi ensemo yaye, na abakungu babo ensemo yabo; eamate ya Simei ensemo yaye, na abakungu babo ensemo yabo; 
chiamate chionsi chiatigaire chieumie, kera eyemo ensemo yaye, na abakungu babo ensemo yabo.
