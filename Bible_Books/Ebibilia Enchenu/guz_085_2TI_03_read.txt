2 TIMOTHEO.
3.
Gaaki, manya ring’ana eri, ng’a ase amatuko y’omoerio inachibe chingaki chi’obokong’u. 
Inaki abanto inababe bakweancha barabwo abanyene, abanchete chitaabu, abetogia, abaenenu, abarami, abatari koigwera abaibori babo, abatari koiraneria ng’a mbuya mono, abatari abachenu, 
abatancheti abanto baande, abatarigeti kobwatana, abang’aachi, abatari gwetanga ase rinde rionsi, abatiindi, abageechete ayare amaya, 
abarua abaande, abatancheti gokanigwa, abamwenu, abanchete kwegokia kobua komwancha Nyasae, 
abakororekana ng’a mbare ne chingeencho chi’obonyasae, korende bakaana okobua gwe chingeencho echio. Bweatanane korwa ase abwo. 
Inaki ase egati yabo mbareo abagwesoyia ase chinyomba chi’abaanto gosakora abakungu aboro, abare ne chigurube chi’ebibe, na koraagwa nokogania okobe gwe chingeencho ao ao. 
Abakungu abwo nigo bakweorokereria botaambe, korende tibakonyara goikera chiseemi chi’ekeene. 
Naboigo buna Yane na Yambare baengereranetie na Musa, na aba nabo bakoengererania nekeene. Ebirengererio biabo biasarekire, na okwegena kwabo kwangirwe, 
korende tibakogeenderera mono, ekiagera oboriri bwabo naboorokigwe maiso marore ase abanto bonsi, buna yabeete ase baria babere. 
Korende aye kwabwatire okworokereria kw’ane, na orogeendo rw’ane, na okorigia kw’ane ase obogima obo, na okwegena kw’ane, na oboremereria bwane, na obwanchani bwane, na okoganya kw’ane, 
na ogochaandwa kw’ane, na ogochaandeka kw’ane, okwo nanyorete ase Antiokia na Ikonio, na Listra. Nogochaandwa kore inaki naremereretie! Na korwa ase ayio onsi Omonene agantooria. 
Ekeene, aba bonsi baganetie koba ne chingeencho chi’obonyasae ase Kristo Yeso inabachaandwe. 
Korende abanto ababe na abang’ainereria mbamentekane kobua ase obobe, bakong’aina na kong’aineka. 
Korende aye, ogenderere ase ayakworokereretigwe na koyaenekia, gokomanya ng’a nkorwa ase ng’o kweorokereretie. 
Inaki korwa obwana bwao nomanyete amariiko amaachenu, ayaraanyare gokong’ainiyia goika onyore ogotooreka ase okwegena kore ase Kristo Yeso. 
Kera ririiko riutire n’Omoika o Nyasae, nario riranyare gotworokereria, na gotokuurera, na gotorongeyia, na gototang’ana ase oboronge, 
erinde omonto o Nyasae abe oikeranigwe, oisanerigwe, anyare gokora kera ogokora okuya.
