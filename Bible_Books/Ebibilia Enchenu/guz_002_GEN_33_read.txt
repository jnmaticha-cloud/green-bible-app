OMOCHAKANO.
33.
Yakobo akaimokereria amaiso aye igoro, akarigereria, akarora Esau ogocha amo nabaanto amagana ane. Erio agaatanana abana, akaa kera omokungu abaye, Lea, na Raeli, na abakungu baria babere abasomba. 
Akabeeka abakungu baria abasomba amo nabana babo batang’ane, na Lea amo nabana baye korwa magega yabo, na Raeli na Yusufu akababeeka magega kegima. 
Ere omonyene agatang’ana bosio bwabo, akaba okona‐gotung’ama inse chintunda isano na ibere goika agaika ase oyomwabo. 
Esau akaminyoka gochia komorotota, akamoboania rigoti riaye, na komoagata, barabwo bakareererana. 
Ekero Esau achia koimokereria amaiso aye igoro, akarora abakungu na abana, akamobooria: “Aba nabang’o more komo?” Yakobo akamoiraneria: “Aba nabana Nyasae ang’eire inche, omosomba oo, ase obuya bwaye.” 
Erio abakungu baria abasomba bagasuka gochia ang’e, kera oyomo nabana baye, bagatung’ama inse. 
Lea nere boigo agasuka gochia ang’e amo n’abana baye, nabarabwo bagatung’ama inse. Magega kegima Yusufu na Raeli bagasuka gochia ang’e, nabarabwo boigo bagatung’ama inse. 
Naende Esau akamobooria: “Emeganda eye yonsi twaumerana gochia aa, neya ki?” Yakobo akamoiraneria: “Engencho yaye inyore ogwancherwa ase obosio bwao, aye omonene one.” 
Esau akamoiraneria: “Oyominto, echi mbwate nching’isaine. Echire echiao, kabe nachirochio.” 
Korende Yakobo akamoteebia: “Taibo, onye nanyorire ogwancherwa ase obosio bwao, koranche obwate ekeegwa eke korwa ase okoboko kwane; naki okorora obosio bwao nigo enga buna okorora obosio bwa Nyasae, ase ogwanchera kwanyancheire inche. 
Koranche oire ebiegwa ebi nakorenteire, ekiagera Nyasae onkoreire obuya obonge, na inche nimbwate ebing’isaine.” Naboigo ere akaebereria Esau na ere agancha, akabiira. 
Erio Esau akamoteebia: “Eta, togende seito, na inche ningotang’ane.” 
Korende Yakobo akamoiraneria: “Omonene one, nomanyete ng’a abana nebing’werere mbwate, naende chimbori ne ching’ondi nigo chibwate chimanwa chikogonka, ne chiombe boigo. Ching’iti echio chiise koayererigwa ase rituko erimo, nigo chirakwe chionsi. 
Koranche, omonene one, oete otang’ane bosio bwane, inche omosomba oo; na inche nindaae ching’iti echio ng’ora ng’ora, buna orogendo rwabo rong’ana, na abana buna chinguru chiabo ching’ana, goika ng’ike bwoo, omonene one, aaria Edomu.” 
Erio Esau akamoteebia: “Ningotigere abanto bande ase aba inde nabwo.” Korende Yakobo akairaneria: “Bakore ki? Otatiga ndore ng’a nanyorire ogwancherwa ase obosio bwao, omonene one.” 
Ase rituko erio erio Esau akabwatia enchera, akairana gochia Edomu. 
Korende Yakobo akagenderera, agaika Sukoti, akeagachera enyomba, akaagachera na amaicho aye ebigutu. Ase igo, aase aria akarokwa Sukoti. 
Yakobo kairanire korwa Padan‐Aramu, agaika buya ase omorembe ase Sekemu, omochie omonene bw’ense ya Kaanani, agatenenia eema yaye isiko y’omochie oyio. 
Korwa ase basinto ba Hamori, ise Sekemu, Yakobo akagora oboremo obwo ase rigori rie chifeta rigana erimo, agwo ase atenenetie eema yaye. 
Akaagacha agwo egesasiimero, agakeroka Eli‐Elohe‐Israeli.
