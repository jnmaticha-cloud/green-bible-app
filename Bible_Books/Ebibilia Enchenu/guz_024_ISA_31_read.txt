ISAYA.
31.
Obobe nobwabo abwo bagotirimboka gochia Misiri korigia ogokonywa, abwo basemeretie chibarasi ne chigari, ekiagera chire chinyinge, na abariini chibarasi, ekiagera bare ne chinguru, korende tibari korigia Oyore Omochenu bw’Abaisraeli, gose korigia ogosesenigwa korwa ase Omonene oyio. 
Ere nigo are omong’aini na nigo akorenta obobe gose tari koonchora amang’ana aye. Nae eamate y’abakori amabe egesusuro, boigo nae abakoonyi b’abagokora amabe egesusuro. 
Abamisiri nigo bare Mwanyabaanto, tibari Nyasae; chibarasi chiabo nigo chire emebere, tichiri emeika. Ekero Omonene ararambore okoboko kwaye, abwo bagokonya mbagochwe, na abwo bagokonywa bagwe; bonsi nigo barasirigwe amo. 
Aya nar’Omonene antebetie; “Buna koreng’ana endo ensae ekoronya ase eng’iti yaitire, na teri kogoswa namariogi ’omoganda bw’abarisia, gose teri kwoboa egeturi kiabo, naboigo inche Omonene bw’Emeganda ndache korwana ase Egetunwa gia Sayoni, na ase ekegoro kiaye. 
Buna koreng’ana chinyoni chikorambora chimbaaba chiabo, naboigo inche Omonene bw’Emeganda ndaegere Yerusalemu; ninyorende na koyobooria; ninyororere amaabera na koyotooria.” 
Inwe Abaisraeli, moiranere oria mwasaretie asare mokamotiga. 
Engaki ngocha ere kera omonto aratugute emegwekano yaye agosasiima eroisirie korwa ase chifeta ne chitaabu, eyio mwaroisetie namaboko aino, na igo mogakora ebibe. 
“Abaasuri mbaitwe nemioro, korende tari eya Mwanyabaanto, ee, emioro etari eya Mwanyabaanto nebasirie. Abanto abwo mbatame korwa ase esegi, na abamura babo mbairwe ase obosomba, bakore emeremo ase okobetererigwa. 
Oyore egetare kiabo natame ore nomoondoko omonge; na abakogaamba amo nere mbatame ase okogoswa ekero bararore ebendera y’esegi.” Omonene, oyio omorero oye ore ase Sayoni, na ekebemberi kiaye ki’omorero kere Yerusalemu, oteebire amang’ana ayio.
