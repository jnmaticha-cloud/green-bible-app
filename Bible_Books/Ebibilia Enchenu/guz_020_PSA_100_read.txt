ZABURI.
100.
(Ogoteera kw’ogoaka ng’a mbuya mono) Inwe abanto b’ense yonsi, moterere Omonene more nomochengo! 
Mokorere Omonene more nomogooko, moche ase obosio bwaye na komoterera amatera ’omochengo! 
Manya ng’a Omonene nere Nyasae. Nere ototongete, na intwe nabaye tore; nigo tore abanto baye, ne ching’ondi chie riicho riaye. 
Soa ase ebiita biaye na komoakera ng’a mbuya mono, soa ase chigoora chiaye na komotogia! Moakere ng’a mbuya mono, motogie erieta riaye! 
Naki Omonene nomuya, na obwanchani bwaye nobwa kare na kare, na obwegenwa bwaye nigo bogendererete ebiaare ase ebiaare.
