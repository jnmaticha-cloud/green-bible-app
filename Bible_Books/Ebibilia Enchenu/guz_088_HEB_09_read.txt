ABAIBERANIA.
9.
Gaki, nonya okobwatana kw’eritang’ani kwarenge nemeroberio ase okomosasiima Nyasae, naende kwarenge nase aachenu ase ense eye. 
Naki eema eria ye ritang’ani yaroisetigwe. Ime ase eema eria mbiarengeo ekebekerero gie chitaaya, na emesa amo nemegati yarenge koruegwa ase Nyasae. Aase aria are korokwa Aase Aachenu. 
Na moino y’etoto eria ya kabere yarengeo eema ende yare korokwa Aase Aachenu Mono. 
Ime aroro mbiarengeo egesasiimero gi’etaabu ki’ogosambera ubani, na esanduki y’okobwatana yaoomire netaabu chinsemo chionsi. Ime ase esanduki eria mbiarengeo: Egetaabo gi’etaabu aase biabegetwe endagera yare korokwa mana, na enyimbo ya Aroni yasebogete, ne chibao chi’amagena aase amang’ana okobwatana kw’ogotianania ariikire. 
Igoro yaye yarengeo emegwekano y’ebitongwa bibwate chimbaba y’obonene yare kobekera egeikaransero ki’amaabera ekiriri. Bono titokonyara goteeba amang’ana ’ebinto ebi ekemo ekemo. 
Ebinto ebi kobiaroisigwe igo, abakuani baare gosoa ase Eema ye ritang’ani ase amatuko onsi na goikerania obokoreri bwabo, 
korende Eema ya kabere Omokuani Omonene ore gosoa bweka ara rimo kera omwaka, na tare konyara gosoa atari namanyinga, ayare korua ase engencho yaye ere omonyene na ase engencho y’amamocho ’abaanto. 
Omoika Omochenu nigo okworokereria ring’ana eri, ng’a enchera y’ogosoa Aase Aachenu konya teraorokigwa ekero Eema ye ritang’ani ereo eteneine. 
Eyio nekiorokererio ase engaki eye ya bono, ekero ebiegwa na ebing’wanso bikorugwa, ebitanyare komoikerania oyokobirua erinde enkoro yaye temogambia. 
Ebinto ebi nigo biare ase okoria na okonywa, na ogosibigwa ao ao, amachiiko ase omobere ayabekire goika engaki y’okoroisigwa okoyia eike. 
Korende Kristo oikire gocha, oyore Omokuani Omonene bw’amang’ana amaya agochi‐gocha. Agaeta ase eema ere enene na eng’ikeranu kobua, etakoretwe namaboko, engencho yaye etari egetongwa gi’ense eye. 
Ere tasoete namanyinga e chimbori na eye chimori, korende namanyinga aye omonyene, agasoa ara rimo rioka Aase Aachenu, na agatonyorera okoboorigwa gwa kare na kare. 
Naki onye amanyinga e chimbori na aye chieri, ne ribu ri’amatororo biare konyororokerigwa ase abatachenegeti mbinyare gochena ase ogosibigwa kw’omobere, 
tari mono kobua amanyinga a Kristo, ere ase Omoika o kare na kare akerua ere omonyene ase Nyasae koba ekeng’wanso getari na komocha, anyare gochena okomanya gwe chinkoro chiaito ime korwa ase emeremo ekwete, erinde tokorere Nyasae ore moyo? 
Ase engencho eye ere nomobwatania bw’okobwatana okoyia, erinde abwo barangerigwe banyore omwando oria o kare na kare, oyo barieretwe eira, ekiagera amakweri aikire gokoreka ase okobabooria barabwo korwa ase ogosaria koria bakorete ase okobwatana kw’eritang’ani. 
Naki aase okoriera kw’omwando kore, goika amakweri aye, oyoriete, abeo. 
Naki okoriera kw’omwando nigo kogoikeranigwa ekero ki’amakweri, ekiagera onye oria oyoriete ore moyo, rirorio tikori na nguru. 
Ase ayio, okobwatana koria kw’eritang’ani tigwachagete gotare namanyinga, 
naki ekero kera richiko riaikire gokwanwa na Musa ase abanto bonsi, buna yachigetwe ase amachiiko, akabogoria amanyinga e chimori na aye chimbori amo namaache na amarionya amabariri, ne chinsagia chia hisopo, akanyororokeria egetabu enyene, na abanto bonsi, 
ogoteeba, “Aya namanyinga ’okobwatana oko Nyasae abachikerete inwe.” 
Eema amo n’ebinto bionsi biare gokorerwa ase ogosasiima naboigo are kobinyororokereria amanyinga. 
Amachiiko nigo are goteeba ng’a ang’e ebinto bionsi bichenwe namanyinga, na amanyinga ataiteiri, okwaberwa kw’ebibe tigokobao. 
Gaki, yagwenerete emegwekano y’ebinto bire igoro bichenwe ase ebing’wanso buna ebio, korende ebinto bia igoro ebinyene bichenwe nebing’wanso bire ebiya kobua ebi. 
Naki Kristo tasoete Aase Aachenu akoretwe namaboko, naro omogwekano bw’Aachenu a ekeene, korende agasoa igoro aanyene, na bono nao akororekana ase obosio bwa Nyasae ase engencho yaito intwe. 
Ere terua omonyene ekero ase ekero, buna Omokuani omonene are gosoa Aase Aachenu kera omwaka amo namanyinga atari ayaye. 
Onye nabo, anga yamogwenerete achandwe ekero ase ekero korwa omochakano, ekero oboroso bw’ense bwabegetwe. Korende bono, ase amatuko ’omoerio bw’ense akaoroka ara rimo rioka ase okorusia ebibe ase okong’wanswa kwaye ere omonyene. 
Buna abanto bagacheirwe ogokwa ara rimo, na magega y’ogokwa ekiina, 
naboigo Kristo nere, gakoorire koruegwa abe ekeng’wanso ara rimo ase okorusia ebibe bi’abaanto abange, narorekane eria kabere, tari ase okobogoria ebibe, korende ase okonyorera abamoganyete ogotooreka.
