ISAYA.
33.
Obobe nobwaino inwe aba mogosiria, ko inwe timwana gosirigwa! Obobe nobwaino inwe abaurereria, ko inwe timwana kourererigwa! Ekero morabe mwakoorire gosiria, nainwe mosirigwe, na ekero morabe mwakoorire kourereria, nainwe mourererigwe. 
Aye Omonene, tororere amaabera; twabeire togosemeretie. Kabe chinguru chiaito kera rituko, na ototoorie ase engaki y’emechando. 
Ekero aye gwakanire neriogi ringa buna eri’okogukura, abanto b’ebisaku nigo bagotama, na ekero kwaimokereire igoro, barabwo nigo bagoserebekana. 
Abanto nigo bagocha baabasakora buna chingeeti chigosakora, na buna chingige chikoumokera, nabarabwo nabo bakobaumokera. 
Omonene nigo aimokererete igoro, ekiagera nigo amenyete igoro mono; naichorie Sayoni nekeene na oboronge. 
Ase chingaki chiria Omonene nabae oborendi obuya, na obonge bw’ogotooreka, na obong’aini, na okomanya; okomoiroka Omonene nakwo korabe enibo yaino. 
Igwa, abanto abwo bare chituoni nigo bakorera mono agwo isiko, na abwo batomirwe bare namang’ana ’omorembe nigo bakorera bare nobororo. 
Chinchera chinene chiasarekire, omoeti tari ogoeta aroro. Okobwatana kw’ogotianania gwasarigwe; emechie yachayirwe, na abanto tibasikiri. 
Ense nigo ekweumia, yaereirwe chinguru; rinani ria Lebanoni riasookire na gosusana; rikura ria Saroni riabeire buna Ekerubo Ekiomo, na emete ya Basani na Karimeli yaarorokire amato. 
Omonene nigo agoteeba iga: “Bono ningororoke ntenene, bono ninyimokerere igoro inyetogie. 
Inwe nigo morabe barito bw’obonyansi obwomo, moibore amasakwe. Omoika oino bw’okoeyana noro omorero orabasambe moere. 
Abanto mbasambwe bayie babe ribu, na buna amagwa akobutorwa asambwa nomorero.” 
Inwe abamomenyete aare, igwa aya nakorire; nainwe abamomenyete ang’e ancha na koorokia okobua kwane. 
Abanyabibe bare Sayoni basoirwe nobwoba; omoondoko obabwatire abwo batamoirogeti Nyasae. “Monto ki oito oranyare koremereria amenye nomorero ogosiria? Ning’o ore ase tore oranyare komenya ime ase omorero oyio ogendererete gwoka kare na kare?” 
Omonto okobwatia oboronge na gokwana amang’ana ’oboronge, oyio ogechete okonyora kw’ogokorera abande bobe na otangete amaboko aye tabwata riaaki, oyio otichete amato aye tabaisa koigwa amang’ana igoro y’ogoitera amanyinga, naende otari korigereria amang’ana ’obobe, 
oyio nere oramenye aase are igoro. Ebitare ebikong’u mbibe ebikengero biaye; naegwe endagera yaye, na amaache aye tagosira. 
Amaiso aino arore omorwoti ore ase obwari bwaye; narore ense ere engare mono. 
Chinkoro chiaino nchirengererie igoro y’obwoba boria na goteeba: “Inee! Oria orenge kobeeka ebango, ng’ai are? Oria orenge korusia ebango, ng’ai are? Oria orenge kobara emerobiri, ng’ai are?” 
Timokorora abanto baria abatindi naende, abanto babwate ogokwana ogokong’u motari komanya, abwo bagokwana emenwa emeao ase okong’ung’ura motari komanya. 
Rigereria Sayoni, omochie oyio ase togosangererekana ase Amatuko aito Amanene! Erio amaiso ao narore Yerusalemu, obomenyo bw’obotoereru, eema etari kona‐kong’anya‐ng’anyigwa; eyio ebirochi biaye bitari gosimorwa, gose chingori chiaye chitari kobutoka. 
Aroro agwo Omonene natoorokere ore nobonene, nabe ase tore buna chindooche na ebirooche, ase amaato agosukigwa nemegango ataete, gose chimeeri chinene chitaete. 
Omonene nere omonachi ebiina oito, nere omogambi oito. Omonene nere omorwoti oito, natotoorie. 
Chingori chi’omoenga bw’emeeri chianyega‐nyegire, tichikonyara gosiberania omoenga bokong’u aase aye, gose korambora ebitambaa. Erio ebinto ebinge mbisakorwe biatananwe, nonya nebisegora mbisakore ebinto. 
Onde tari bw’omochie oyio orateebe: “Inche nigo nindwarete”, ekiagera abanto abamenyete aroro mbabe baabeirwe obobe bwabo.
