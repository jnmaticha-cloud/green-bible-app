OKOMAANOKA.
13.
Erio nkarora eng’iti egosoka korwa ase enyancha. Ero nigo yarenge ne ching’unchara ikomi na emetwe etano na ebere. Igoro ase ching’unchara chiaye nchiarengeo chigutwa ikomi, na ase emetwe yaye ndiarengeo erieta ri’okoraama. 
Na eng’iti eria narooche nigo yagwekaine engo. Amagoro aye nigo arenge buna ay’edubu, na omonwa oye buna oyo bw’endo. Enchoka ekaa eng’iti eria chinguru chiaye, na ekerogo kiaye ki’obonene, na okobua okonene. 
Omotwe oyomo bw’emetwe y’eng’iti eria okarorekana buna nore norogoma rw’amakweri, korende orogoma rwaye rw’amakweri rokagwenigwa, na ense yonsi egakumia ase eng’iti eria na koyetunyana. 
Abanto bagasasiima enchoka eria, ekiagera yaete eng’iti eria okobua. Naende bagasasiima eng’iti eria bagoteeba, “Ning’o oreng’aine neng’iti eye? Ning’o oranyare koyerwania?” 
Eng’iti eria ekaegwa ribaga ri’ogokwana amang’ana amanene na ay’okorama, naende ekaegwa okobua gw’okogamba ase engaki y’emetienyi emerongo ene na ebere. 
Na igo egachaaka korama Nyasae, ekarama na erieta riaye, na obomenyo bwaye, na abwo bamenyete igoro, 
naende ekaegwa ribaga korwania abachenu na kobabua. Ekaegwa okobua igoro ya kera eamate, na abanto, na omonwa, na egesaku. 
Abanto bonsi abamenyete ase ense mbayesasime, otatiga abwo amarieta abo ariikire ase egetabu y’obogima y’Emanwa y’Eng’ondi, oyoitetwe korwa omochakano, ekero oboroso bw’ense bwabegetwe. 
Omonto kare namato akoigwera, tiga aigwe: 
Omonto gachoreirwe ogwoswa, goika oswe. Omonto gachoreirwe ogoitwa nomoyio o birende, goika aitwe nomoyio o birende. Aiga nao abachenu barabe nokoremereria na obwegenwa. 
Erio nkarora eng’iti ende egotiira korwa inse. Ero nigo yarenge ne ching’unchara ibere chinga buna echi’emanwa y’eng’ondi, egakwana buna enchoka. 
Nigo ekwenaria okobua gwonsi kw’eng’iti eria ye ritang’ani ase obosio bwaye, na kogera ense na bonsi abamenyete aroro basasime eng’iti eria ye ritang’ani, ero eyio orogoma rwaye rw’amakweri rwagwenetigwe. 
Nero yakorire ebikone ebinene, goika yagerire omorero oike korwa igoro gocha ase ense ase amasio a Mwanyabaanto. 
Na ase ebikone biria yaetwe ribaga kobikora ase obosio bw’eng’iti eria ye ritang’ani ekong’aina abwo bamenyete ase ense, egoteebia abamenyete ase ense bakore omogwekano bw’eng’iti eria yaagetwe orogoma nomoyio o birende, nero ekaba moyo. 
Ekaegwa ribaga kobekera omogwekano bw’eng’iti eria omoika, goika omogwekano oria okwane, na ogere abanto bonsi abatasasimete omogwekano oria bw’eng’iti baitwe. 
Naboigo ekagera abanto bonsi, abake na abanene, abanda na abataka, abatari abasomba na abasomba, babekwe ekemanyererio ase amaboko abo a borio gose amasio abo igoro, 
erinde monto onde bwensi tagora gose koonia otatiga ere oyore nekemanyererio keria, engencho yaye erieta ri’eng’iti eria gose chinamba chi’erieta riaye. 
Aiga nao obong’aini boganeirie. Ere oyore ne chisemi tiga abare chinamba chi’eng’iti eyio, naki echi nechinamba chia Mwanyabaanto, ne chinamba chiaye namagana atano na rimo na emerongo etano nomo na isano nemo (666).
