ZABURI.
133.
(Ogoteera kwa Daudi) Rora buna ere buya na okogokia okonene ekero abanto abamo bakomenya amo, baigwanaine. 
Amang’ana ayio nigo anga buna amaguta akweakwa are amaya mono, akonyororoka gochia ase chinderu chia Aroni, aitekera omokoba bwe rigoti ri’omwambaro oye. 
Naende nigo anga buna riime ria Herimoni, rigotonya igoro y’ebitunwa bia Sayoni. Aroro agwo Omonene ochiikire ogosesenigwa na obogima botari nomoerio.
