ABALAWI.
15.
Omonene agakwana na Musa na Aroni, akabateebia, 
“Kwana n’Abaisraeli, mobateebie ng’a onye omonto omosacha okorua amaache anga buna chimengere korwa ase ekiimo kiaye ki’obosacha, amaache ayio neubi. 
Aya naro achiikire ase amang’ana ayio: Amaache ayio karagenderere korua, gose atigire korua, omonto oyio tachenegeti. 
Ebirarero bionsi araararere, gose araikaransere, nigo biraabe ebitachenegeti. 
Monto onde bwensi aise gokuna ebiraarero bi’omonto oyio goika asibie chianga chiaye, esibie nomobere namaache, ere nabe oyotachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio. 
Na onde bwensi oraikaransere egento omonto oria omorwaire aikaranserete, goika asibie chianga chiaye, esibie nomobere namaache, ere nabe oyotachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio. 
Na onde bwensi orakune omonto oyio omorwaire goika asibie chianga chiaye, esibie nomobere namaache, ere nabe oyotachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio. 
Omonto oyio omorwaire karatwere omonto ochenegete amate, nere goika asibie chianga chiaye, esibie nomobere namaache, naende abe oyotachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio. 
Egeikaransero gekobekerwa eng’iti ekoriinwa, gokeraikaranserwe nomonto oria omorwaire, nakebe egetachenegeti. 
Omonto aise gokuna egento kende gionsi kiareenge inse y’omonto oria omorwaire nabe oyotachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio; na onde bwensi oraimokie egento buna ekio nigo arasibie chianga chiaye, esibie nomobere namaache, ere nabe oyotachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio. 
Onye omonto oria omorwaire ogokuna omonto onde ataraisaba amaboko, oria okunwa goika asibie chianga chiaye, esibie nomobere namaache naende nabe oyotachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio. 
Enyongo ende yonsi omonto oria omorwaire arakune goika yaatwe, na egekorero kende gionsi keroiseirie omote arakune goika gesibigwe namaache. 
“Omonto oria gachenekire korwa ase okorua kw’amaache aria anga buna achimengere goika abare amatuko atano na abere ay’ogocheneka kwaye. Erio asibie chianga chiaye, esibie nomobere namaache amaya a rooche, ere nabe oyochenegete. 
Ase rituko ria gatano na gatato aire amaruma abere, gose amage ’amaruma abere, ase obosio bwane inche Omonene agwo ase egesoero ki’Eema y’Omosangererekano, ayae Omokuani. 
Omokuani arue erimo ribe ekeng’wanso ki’ebibe, na erinde ribe ekeng’wanso ki’ogosaambwa. Ase enchera eyio akore okobwatanigwa ase omonto oria ase obosio bwane, ase engencho y’okorua kwaye kw’amaache anga buna chimengere. 
“Onye omonto omosacha ogosokia embusuro yaye y’oroiboro, rirorio esibie omobere namaache, na agenderere koba oyotachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio. 
Eanga ende yonsi, na ebinto binde bionsi bi’amasankwa, embusuro eyio y’oroiboro eratonyere, goika bisibigwe namaache, nabirobio nabibe ebitachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio. 
Omonto onye orarire n’omokuungu, na omobekeire embusuro y’oroiboro, bonsi babere goika besibie emebere yabo namaache, nabarabwo nababe abatachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio. 
“Ekero omokungu are nomotienyi nigo arabe oyotachenegeti ase engaki y’amatuko atano na abere. Onde bwensi oramokune nabe oyotachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio. 
Gento kende gionsi arabe okoraarera ekero are nomotienyi, nigo keraabe egetachenegeti, boigo n’ebinto bionsi araikaransere. 
Onde bwensi orakune ebiraarero bi’omokuungu oyio goika asibie chianga chiaye, esibie nomobere namaache, ere nabe oyotachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio. 
Na onde bwensi orakune egento omokungu oyio aikaranserete, nigo arasibie chianga chiaye, esibie nomobere namaache; ere nabe oyotachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio. 
Ase igo kobiraabe ebiraarero, gose egento omokungu oria aikaranserete, omonto karabikune nabe oyotachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio. 
Omosacha karaarare n’omokuungu ore nomotienyi, ogotacheneka kw’omokuungu oyio nakobe asare, ere nabe oyotachenegeti ase engaki y’amatuko atano na abere, na gento kende gionsi araararere nakebe egetachenegeti. 
“Omokungu karagenderere goiteka amanyinga ase amatuko amange atari ay’omotienyi oye, gose ayagoiteka goetania amatuko aye ay’omotienyi, nigo arabe oyotachenegeti ase engaki eyio yonsi arabe ogoiteka amanyinga; nabe oyotachenegeti buna ekero are nomotienyi. 
Ebiraarero bionsi are koraarera ekero agoiteka amanyinga, nigo biraabe ebitachenegeti buna ebi akoraarera ekero are nomotienyi, naboigo na kende gionsi araikaransere. 
Na onde bwensi orakune ebinto ebio nabe oyotachenegeti; ere asibie chianga chiaye, esibie nomobere namaache, naende nabe oyotachenegeti goika omogoroba oyio. 
Ekero ogoiteka kwaye kw’amanyinga gwakamire, abare amatuko atano na abere, erio nabe ochenekire. 
Ase rituko ria gatano na gatato aire amaruma abere gose amage ’amaruma abere ase Omokuani agwo ase egesoero ki’Eema y’Omosangererekano. 
Omokuani arue erimo ribe ekeng’wanso ki’ebibe, na erinde eki’ogosaambwa. Ase enchera eyio nakore okobwatanigwa ase omokungu oria ase obosio bwane, ase engencho y’ogotacheneka kwaye kw’ogoiteka kw’amanyinga. 
“Ase enchera eyio okuurere Abaisraeli igoro y’ogotacheneka kwabo, tibaabaisa kogera Obomenyo bwane Obochenu bore ase egati yabo bobe obotachenegeti. Baise gokora bo, nigo barakwe.” 
Aya naro amachiiko ase omonto okorua amaache anga buna chimengere, na oyogosokia embusuro yaye y’oroiboro, na koba oyotachenegeti ase engencho eyio, 
naboigo nase omokungu ore nomotienyi, na ase onde bwensi, omosacha okorua amaache anga buna chimengere, na omokungu ogoiteka amanyinga, naende ase omosacha okoraara n’omokuungu otachenegeti.
