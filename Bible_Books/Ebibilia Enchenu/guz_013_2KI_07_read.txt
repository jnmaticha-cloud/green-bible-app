2 ABARWOTI.
7.
Elisha akamoiraneria: “Igwa ring’ana ri’Omonene. Aya nar’Omonene agoteeba: Ankio, engaki buna eye obosie obonuke bw’engano ebitita bibere nabionigwe ase rigori ri’esekeli eyemo, na obw’esairi ebitita bine ase esekeli eyemo, agwo ase egeita gia Samaria.” 
Erio omotang’ani bw’abasikari, oyio omorwoti egenete, agateebia omonto oria o Nyasae: “Nony’Omonene omonyene are koigora ebibao bia igoro, egento ekio tigekonyara koba.” Korende omonto oria o Nyasae agateeba: “Aye noyarore namaiso ao, korende tokoria endagera eyio.” 
Bono abanto baane barwarete oborwaire bwa ukoma mbaarenge agwo ase egesoero ki’egeita. Barabwo bagateebania: “Ninki gekogera toikaranse aiga goika tokwe? 
Toise goteeba ng’a tosoe omochie ime, enchara nereo, nabo torakwe; na toise goikaransa aiga, boigo ntokwe. Ase ayio togende goika ase egetwori ki’Abasiiria. Kobaratotige, tobe moyo, ntogenderere koba moyo; kobaratoite ntobe twakuure.” 
Bakaimoka mambia ense etarakiamoka, bakagenda ase egetwori ki’Abasiiria, korende ekero baigete ase omochakano bw’egetwori keria bakanyora ng’a monto taiyo. 
Omonene ogerire omoganda bw’abasikari b’Abasiiria baigure ebititiro bie chigari, ne chibarasi, na eriogi ri’omoganda omonene bw’abasikari. Ase ayio konya bateebanirie: “Ekeene, omorwoti bw’Abaisraeli osabire abarwoti b’Abahiti na baria b’Abamisiri bache gotorwania.” 
Erio bagatama ense etarakiamoka, bagatiga chiema chiabo, ne chibarasi chiabo, ne chitigere chiabo; bagatiga negetwori, bagatama ase ogotooria obogima bwabo. 
Ekero abanto baria barwarete oborwaire bwa ukoma baigete ase omochakano bw’egetwori bagasoa ase eema eyemo, bakaria endagera na konywa, bakabogoria chifeta, ne chitaabu, ne chianga, bakagenda bagachibisa. Naende bakairana, bagasoa ase eema ende, bakabogoria ebinto korwa aroro, bakagenda nabirobio bakabibisa. 
Erio bagateebania: “Aya tari aro amaronge togokora. Rituko ria reero neri’amang’ana amaya. Onye togokira na koganya goika bokie, ekeene, egesusuro ngetonyore. Ase ayio, inchwo togende, totebie abanto b’enyomba y’omorwoti amang’ana aya.” 
Bakagenda, bakarangeria abarendi b’egeita ki’omochie, bakabateebia: “Iga twagenda ase egetwori ki’Abasiiria, twanyora ng’a monto onde tario nonya noyomo, gose titoigweti riogi ria monto; twanyora chibarasi chisibire, ne chitigere boigo, ne chiema chiteneine buna bachitigete.” 
Erio abarendi b’egeita bakararia amang’ana ayio nonya ngoikera ase enyomba y’omorwoti. 
Na omorwoti akaimoka botuko, agateebia abasomba baye: “Tiga mbateebie egento Abasiiria barobeirie mamincha yaito. Bamanyire ng’a intwe igo tore nenchara; ase ayio basokire korwa ase egetwori bagendire kwebisa orosana batoboere, baarengereretie ng’a ekero togosoka korwa ase omochie batobwate tore moyo, naende basoe omochie bayobwate.” 
Oyomo bw’abasomba baye agateeba: “Tiga totome abanto bagende boyie chibarasi isano korwa ase echio chitigarete ase omochie, baroote tomanye amang’ana buna are. Emechando erabanyore abanto abwo tekoba emebe kobua ey’abwo batigarete ase omochie, gose ey’Abaisraeli bonsi basiririe obogima bwabo. 
Ase igo bagachora abanto babere bakariina chigari chiare kong’uswa ne chibarasi, na omorwoti akabatoma gochia koroota abasikari b’Abasiiria.” 
Ase ayio bakababwatia goika Yorodani, bakarora chianga na ebinto binde Abasiiria batugutete inchera ekero baarenge gotama. Abanto baria batometwe bakairana, bagateebia omorwoti amang’ana ayio. 
Abanto ba Samaria bakagenda bagasakora egetwori ki’Abasiiria. Ase ayio obosie obonuke bw’engano ebitita bibere bokaoonigwa ase esekeli eyemo, na obw’esairi ebitita bine ase esekeli eyemo, buna Omonene ateebete. 
Bono omorwoti konya ochorire omotang’ani oria bw’abasikari, ere omonyene egenete, abe omorendi bw’egeita. Abanto bakamotindachera agwo ase egeita goika agakwa, buna omonto oria o Nyasae ateebete ekero omorwoti achete asare. 
Nigo abeete buna omonto oria o Nyasae atebeetie omorwoti: “Engaki buna eye ankio agwo ase egeita gia Samaria obosie bw’esairi ebitita bine na bioonigwe ase esekeli eyemo, na obosie obonuke bw’engano ebitita bibere ase esekeli eyemo.” 
Omotang’ani oria bw’abasikari nigo airaneretie omonto oria o Nyasae, agateeba: “Nony’Omonene omonyene are koigora ebibao bia igoro, ayio takonyara koba.” Omonto oria o Nyasae nigo amoiraneretie: “Aye noyarore namaiso ao, korende tokoria endagera eyio.” 
Ayio agakoreka asare, na abanto igo bamotindacherete agwo ase egeita, agakwa.
