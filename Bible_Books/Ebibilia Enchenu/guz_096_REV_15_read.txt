OKOMAANOKA.
15.
Erio nkarora egekone kende igoro, ekenene na eki’ogokumia. Abamalaika batano na babere baare namaakwa atano na abere, ayio nay’omoerio, ekiagera ase ayio endamwamu ya Nyasae yaikeranigwe. 
Naende nkarora aase arenge buna enyancha y’ekerore, esang’anaine nomorero. Naboigo nkarora abwo babuire eng’iti na omogwekano oye, na enamba y’erieta riaye. Barabwo nigo bateneine ensemo y’enyancha y’ekerore, mbabwate amakano aya baetwe na Nyasae. 
Na barabwo nigo baare goteera ogoteera kwa Musa, omosomba o Nyasae, na ogoteera kw’Emanwa y’Eng’ondi, bagoteeba, “Ogokora kwao nokonene na okw’ogokumia, aye Omonene Omobui! Chinchera chiao nechi’oboronge na echi’ekeene, aye omorwoti bw’abaanto b’ebisaku. 
Aye Omonene, ning’o otakoiroke? Ning’o otatogie erieta riao? Naki naye bweka ore omochenu. Abanto b’ebisaku bionsi nabachiche gosasiima ase obosio bwao, ekiagera emeroberio yao y’oboronge yaikire koorokigwa.” 
Magega ase aya nkarora Hekalu ya igoro, eyere Eema y’Oborori, ekoigorwa, 
na abamalaika baria batano na babere, abaare namaakwa atano na abere, bagasooka korwa ase Hekalu. Barabwo nigo babeekeire chianga chi’egatani endabu, ekomeka‐meka, na ase ebikuba biabo nigo beboeretie chinkini chi’echitaabu. 
Na eyemo ye ching’iti chiria inye chire moyo ekaa abamalaika baria batano na babere ebitaabo bitano na bibere bie chitaabu biichire endamwamu ya Nyasae ore moyo goika kare na kare. 
Na Hekalu egaichorwa nerioki rikorwa ase obonene bwa Nyasae, na korwa ase okobua kwaye; monto onde tarenge ore konyara gosoa ase Hekalu goika amaakwa aria atano na abere ay’abamalaika baria batano na babere aikeranigwe.
