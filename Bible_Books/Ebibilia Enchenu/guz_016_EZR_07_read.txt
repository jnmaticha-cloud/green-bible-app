EZRA.
7.
Magega y’amang’ana aria kaerire ase engaki y’okogamba kw’Aritasasita, omorwoti bw’ense y’Ayemi, narengeo omonto okorokwa Ezra, mosinto o Seraya, bw’Asaria, o Hilikia, 
o Salumu, o Zadoki, bw’Ahitubu, 
bw’Amaria, bw’Asaria, o Merayoti, 
o Seraya, o Usi, o Buki, 
bw’Abisua, o Finehasi, bw’Eleazari, o Aroni, Omokuani omonene. 
Ezra oyio akarua Babiloni. Ere nigo arenge omworokia bw’amariko; nigo orokereretigwe na omanyete mono amachiiko a Musa, ayio Omonene, Nyasae bw’Abaisraeli, aruete; na ekiagera okoboko kw’Omonene, Nyasae oye, kwarenge amo nere, omorwoti akamoa gento kende gionsi aboretie. 
Ase omwaka o gatano na kabere bw’okogamba kw’Aritasasita omorwoti, abande b’Abaisraeli, na abakuani, na Abalawi, na abateri, na abarendi b’ebiita, na abakori emeremo be Hekalu, bakagenda Yerusalemu. 
Bono ase omwaka o gatano na kabere, omotienyi o gatano, bw’okogamba kw’omorwoti oria, Ezra agaika Yerusalemu. 
Nigo achagete orogendo rwaye korwa Babiloni ase rituko rie ritang’ani ri’omotienyi bw’eritang’ani, na agaika Yerusalemu rituko rie ritang’ani ri’omotienyi o gatano, ekiagera okoboko kwa Nyasae oye kwarenge amo nere ase okomokonya. 
Ezra konya orure enkoro yaye kweorokereria amachiiko ’Omonene na koyakora, naende nigo arenge kworokereria Abaisraeli emeroberio yaye, na okogamba kwaye kwa boronge. 
Amang’ana aya naro ariikire ase riruube riria omorwoti Aritasasita aete Ezra, Omokuani na omworokia bw’amariko, oyo bworokereretigwe na omanyete mono amachiiko ’Omonene na emeroberio yaye echiikire ase Abaisraeli. 
“Inche Aritasasita, omorwoti bw’abarwoti, ngokorikera inde aye Ezra Omokuani, aye kwegerete amachiiko a Nyasae o igoro. Amakwania amo nayakobwatia ayio. 
“Nachikiire ng’a monto onde bwensi bw’Abaisraeli, gose oyo bw’abakuani babo, gose oyo bw’Abalawi, oyomenyete ase oborwoti bwane, karanche kogenda Yerusalemu amo naye, tiga agende. 
“Inche omorwoti, amo nabasemia baane batano na babere, twagotomire ogende komanya amang’ana ’ense y’Abayuda, na aya Yerusalemu, kobwatekana namachiiko a Nyasae oo, ayio obwate ase okoboko kwao. 
Naende oire chifeta ne chitaabu chiria inche omorwoti, na abasemia baane, twarure nogwancha ase Nyasae bw’Abaisraeli, oyio obomenyo bwaye bore Yerusalemu. 
Boigo oire chifeta ne chitaabu chionsi, echio oranyore korwa ase ense yonsi ya Babiloni, oire nokoruegwa kw’ogwancha okwo abanto na abakuani bararue ase enyomba ya Nyasae obo ere Yerusalemu. 
“Obe omwegenwa, na ase chitaabu echio ogore chieri, ne chimingichi, ne chimanwa chi’eching’ondi, na okoruegwa kw’endagera, na okw’ebinyugwa bikoruegwa amo nebing’wanso ebio. Orue ebinto ebio ase egesasiimero ki’enyomba ya Nyasae oo ere agwo Yerusalemu. 
Emeremo ende yonsi aye amo nabaanto bamino morarore ere buya, chifeta n’echitaabu chiratigare mochiyekorere, korende mokore igo kobwatekana nogwancha kwa Nyasae oino. 
Ebikorero ebio kwaeirwe gokorera emeremo ase enyomba ya Nyasae oo, obiire bionsi, obibeke ase obosio bwa Nyasae agwo Yerusalemu. 
Ebinto binde bionsi biganeirie ase enyomba ya Nyasae oo, ebio ogwenerete korenta aroro, obigore ne chitaabu chikorwa ase ekungo y’omorwoti. 
“Naende inche omorwoti Aritasasita narure ogochika ase abagachi bonsi be chikungo bare ase orogongo rore bosongo y’orooche rw’Eufarati ng’a, gento kende gionsi Ezra Omokuani oyo bwegerete amachiiko a Nyasae o igoro araganie korwa ase more, mogekorere ase oboronge. 
Momoe goikera chifeta chitalanta rigana erimo (100), na engano ebitonga elifu eyemo (1,000), na edivai chilita chilifu isato, na amagana atano na rimo (3,600), na amaguta ’amazeituni chilita chilifu isato, na amagana atano na rimo (3,600), na momoe omonyoo otari na kerengo. 
Gento kende gionsi Nyasae o igoro arachike ng’a gekorwe, ekio gekorwe boronge ase engencho y’enyomba ya Nyasae o igoro, erinde endamwamu ya Nyasae tebaisa koba igoro y’oborwoti bwane, na abamura baane. 
Naende nigo tokobamanyia ng’a timobaisa kobeeka ebango gose ogochoga ase onde bwensi bw’abakuani, gose Abalawi, gose abateri, gose abarendi b’ebiita, gose abakori emeremo be Hekalu, gose abakori emeremo bande b’enyomba ya Nyasae. 
“Bono aye Ezra kwaeirwe obong’aini na Nyasae oo. Obokorere emeremo ase ogochora abagaambi ebiina na abamanyi b’amachiiko, erinde babe bakogamba ebiina bi’abaanto bonsi ase orogongo rore bosongo y’orooche rw’Eufarati, aba bonsi bamanyete amachiiko a Nyasae oo; na abatayamanyeti oborokererie bayamanye. 
Monto onde bwensi oraange koigwera amachiiko a Nyasae oo, na ay’omorwoti, oyio anacherwe ekiina kia boronge, kebe eki’ogokwa, gose eki’ogoserigwa, gose kourwa ebinto biaye, gose asibwe.” 
Omonene, Nyasae bwe chisokoro chiaito, atogigwe, oria obegete ring’ana eri ime ase enkoro y’omorwoti, oyobwanchire gokora enyomba y’Omonene ere Yerusalemu ebe ensiani mono. 
Ere Omonene oorokirie ase ’nde obwanchani bwaye botari koera ase obosio bw’omorwoti, na ase abasemia baye, na abanene bonsi bare gokonya omorwoti bare nokobua. Inche nkaba noboremu, ekiagera okoboko kw’Omonene, Nyasae one, kwarenge amo nainche, na igo ngasangereria abatang’ani b’Abaisraeli batiire amo nainche.
