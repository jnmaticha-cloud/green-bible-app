Obong’a.
14.
Boigo nabo okonyora omonto otagete korina emeri ase emerindo emenge ogosaba obokonyi korwa ase egetugi ekio getari n’echinguru goetania emeri eria atagete korina! 
Omonto gete nigo aroisetie emeri eyio erio kwenyorera engeria, omoroisia akayeroisia ase obomanyi bwaye. 
Korende aye Omonene oborendi bwao nabwo bokoraa emeri ayio. Ekiagera nonya nyancha aye nigo okoroisia enchera, nonya nase emerindo abanto nigo bagoeta buya; 
koorokia buna aye nabo ogotooria korwa ase kera ase, nonya n’omonto otabwati obomanyi obwo nabo agotara ase amaache. 
Nigo kwarigetie ebinto ebio aye gwatongete bibwenere Mwanyabaanto; abanto nigo bakorora buna nigo bare buya nonya nase ekengese ki’omote, nigo bagotamboka nyancha buya ase ekengese ki’omote na baika ngumbu ende buya. 
Aya naro akorekanete agwo ritangani, ekero amamonto gete amangainereria arenge gosira, omonto oria orenge ogosemeria gw’ense atamete gochia ase obwato inse y’oborai bwao agatigera ense oroiboro ri’ekegori ekeyia. 
Ogosesenigwa kogacha ase obwato boria bwa Nuhu obwo bwaretete ogotoreka. 
Korende okoragererigwa kwabeire ase emegwekano eyio eroisirie n’amaboko a Mwanyabaanto; boigo nonya noria oreisirie emegwekano eyio oragererigwe, oyio nigo aragererigwe ase engencho ere aroisirie omobwekano oyio otari na bomoyo bonde naende oyoroka e nyasae. 
Nyasae nigo agechete abanyabibe boigo nigo agechete ogokora kwabo. 
Keria kiarosigwe boigo noria okeroisirie mbaegwe egesusuro. 
Emegwekano y’ogosasomwa eyio baria batamomanyeti Nyasae baroisirie neegwe egesusuro, nonya keroisirie korwa ase ebinto ebio Nyasae atongete, yabeire okogechia okonene, na yabeire egetango ase emioyo y’abanto, na yabeire omoeto ase amagoro y’ abanto abariri. 
Obonyaka nigo bwachagete ekero emegwekano y’ogosasimwa yachagete koroisigwa. Yaochoganirie ebirengererio bia Mwanyabaanto korwa eroisigwa. 
Mebwekano tiyarengeo korwa kare naende tekobao kare na kare. 
Nigo yaretetwe ense goetera ase obwenenu bwa Mwanyabaanto, nakio gekogera omoerio oye obwango mono oroisigwe. 
Omogaka oyomo agaichanigwa mono n’ogokwa kwa bwango mono kw’omwana oye, erio agwo akaroisia omobwekano bw’omwana oye oria okwete buna Mwanyabaanto onde bwensi agokwa akamoa amasikani buna Nyasae; erio akaa abanto abwo barenge inse yaye omoroberio bw’ogosasiima omobwekano oyio aroisetie. 
Magega yaye, ebirengererio ebio ebibe bi’ogosasiima emegwekano bikaba n’echinguru goika bikaba buna ogochika, na emegwekano y’ogosasimwa ekaroisigwa abanto bagachiikwa koyesasiima. 
Ekaba buna ekero abanto bamenyete are korwa ase omogambi obo oyio basigete mono, nigo bare korengereria buna ekieni kiaye kere erio baroisia omobwekano oye na koyosasiima nonya kare are nabarabwo goetera ase omobwekano nigo bakorengereria buna nao are ange nabarabwo. 
Abaroisia aba b’emegwekano nigo bare koroisia omobwekano omuya mono erio nonya mbaria batamomanyete bakogokigwa nere. 
Omoroisia kero kende nabo akogania kogokia abanene baye ase okoroisia omobwekano omuya mono goetania nonya n’omonene oye. 
Abanto nigo bakogokigwa n’obuya bw’emeremo eyio, erio barora emegwekano eyio koba egento kebwenerete gosasimwa oyio ase ekero egeke basigete buna Mwanyabaanto. 
Bono eke nigo gekoba omoeto ogotega abanto, ekiagera ekero abanto bare ase emechando gose ekero omogambi ekogamba n’echinguru ere, nigo akoira ebinto ebio biroisirie korwa ase amagena gose emete obiaa amasikani ayio Nyasae bweka aganeirie koegwa! 
Korende bono amang’ana akaba amabe mono. Bono tiyarenge bobe ase bare gotatwara obomanyi igoro ya Nyasae. Abanto nigo bamenyete ase obobe obonene n’echisegi ase oboriri goika bagakaga nga igo nario bare n’omorembe. 
Bagaita abana baabo ase ebingwanso bia bobisi, bagakora ebinto bi’obobarimo n’amang’ana ande arenge mamincha y’obotongi. 
Bakeba amagima abo nonya n’echinywomo chiabo tibachisigete. Omonto nigo arenge goita oyonde bosa igo gose omogechia mono ase okorara na mokaye. 
Kera egento gekaba ekebe, abanto bagaitwa bosa igo, oboibi bogaichora, obongainereria, oborianania, obwegenwa bokabora, okoamererania, oborimo, 
okorianania baria batabwati komocha, abanto bagatiga goaka mbuya mono, emeburukano ase chinywomo, obonyaka n’obotomani. 
Gosasiima emegwekano eyio nonya n’amarieta abo atari gwatorwa, nakio omochakano, na engencho ya kera obobe. 
Abanto bagosasiima emegwekano eyio nigo baborirwe obongaini, gose nigo bakobana oborimo, gose bamenya obogima obobe na gotiana oborimo batari korengereria. 
Abanto nigo basemeretie emegwekano eyio etari moyo na gotiana ogotiana okobe barengereria buna tibagocha konyora egesusuro. 
Korende goika banyore egesusuro ase chinchera echi ibere: Ritangani nigo bakorete okomocha ekero batigete Nyasae bagachaaka gosasiima emegwekano, eriakabere tibakumeretie obochenu, nigo bare gokwana amang’ana y’oborimo erio kongaina abanto. 
Ekero abakori amabe bakomocha, nigo baranyore egesusuro tari korwa ase chinguru chi’ ebinto bagotiana, korende korwa ase egesusuro kebatungetie barabwo.
