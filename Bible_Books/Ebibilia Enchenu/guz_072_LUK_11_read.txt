LUKA.
11.
Ngosaba arenge aase gete. Gakoorire, oyomo bw’aborokigwa baye akamoteebia, “Omonene, twegerie ogosaba buna Yohana aegeretie aborokigwa baye.” 
Akabateebia, Komogosaba, teba iga: Tata, Erieta riao risikwe, Oborwoti bwao boche, 
Otoe kera rituko endagera yaito. 
Otwabere ebibe biaito, ekiagera intwe tokwabera kera oyomo otokorire bobe. Totoira ase ogoteemwa. 
Akabateebia, “Ning’o ase more ore nomosani oramochiere botuko gati amoteebie: Mosani, ing’a singo emegati etato, 
ekiagera omosani one ochire ase ’nde korwa orogendo, na inche tindi na gento nkomoa; 
na oria ore ime namoiranerie, na goteeba: Tondosia, egesieri giasiekirwe, na inche amo nabana baane twararire borere, tinkonyara koimoka nkoe? 
Nabatebirie, nataimoka amoe ekiagera nomosani oye, korende ekiagera oria tari gotiga ogosaba, naimoke na komoa ebio bionsi arigetie. 
Na inche nkobateebia nde inwe: Saba, namoegwe; rigia, namonyore; tura, namoigorerwe. 
Ekiagera kera oyogosaba, nigo akoegwa, na ere oyokorigia, nigo akonyora, na ere oyogotura, nigo akoigorerwa. 
Naki monto onde nare ase more oyore ise, na omwana oye amosabe omogati, Inee, namoe rigena? Gose karamosabe enswe, ase ribaga ri’enswe namoe eng’iti? 
Gose karamosabe rigena ri’engoko namoe riagaga? 
Onye inwe, abamore ababe, momanyete koa abana baino ebiegwa ebiya, Inee, Iso oino ore igoro takomenta mono kobaa bonsi, abakomosaba, Omoika Omochenu?” 
Bono ngoseria arenge ekerecha ekio giakorete omonto oyomo koba rimama. Ekero ekerecha keria giasogete, omonto oria rimama agakwana; abanto bagakumia. 
Abande babo bagateeba, “Nase Beelizebuli, omonene bw’ebirecha, akorusia ebirecha.” 
Abande ase okomoteema bakarigia aborokie egekone buna ekemanyererio korwa igoro. 
Ere konya omanyire ebirengererio biabo, akabateebia, “Kera oborwoti kobwatananekanire borobwo obonyene nigo b’ogosareka, na enyomba yaatananekire ero enyene nigo ekogwa. 
Naboigo Saitani karaatananeke ere omonyene, oborwoti bwaye naki boratenene? Naki inwe iga mogoteeba ng’a nigo ngoseria ebirecha ase Beelizebuli. 
Na inche onye ngoseria ebirecha ase Beelizebuli, Inee, abana baino nase ng’o bakobiseria? Ase engencho eyio, barabwo nababe abagaambi baino. 
Korende kondaserie ebirecha ase ekiara kia Nyasae, nabo oborwoti bwa Nyasae bwabacheire. 
Omonto bw’echinguru ore n’ebinto bi’esegi, gakorenda enyomba yaye, enibo yaye nigo ekoba ase omorembe, 
korende oyore ne chinguru kobua, ere karamochiere na komobua, nigo akomoura ebinto biaye bi’esegi bionsi ebiegenerete, na ebisakorwa biaye obiatanana. 
Omonto otari amo nainche nigo are mamincha ase ’nde, na ere oyotari gosangereria amo nainche nigo agoserebania. 
“Ekero ekerecha ki’eubi kiarure ase omonto, nigo kegoeta gati aase atari namaache, gekorigia aase kegotimokera; getanyoreti kiabora: Ning’irane ase enyomba yane ase narwete. 
Gakegocha, kiayenyora yaabusirwe, yaroisigwe. 
Erio kiagenda, kiarentana ebirecha bitano na bibere binde ebire ebibe gokebua kerokio ekenyene. Birobio bigosoa na komenya aroro; okoba kw’omoerio kw’omonto oyio kwaba okobe kobua okwe ritang’ani.” 
Ekero agokwana aya, omokungu oyomo ase omoganda agaaka eriogi, akamoteebia, “Nesesenirie enda yakobogoretie, ne chimbeere kwagongete!” 
Korende Yeso akabora, “Mbuya kobua, basesenirie abakoigwa ring’ana ria Nyasae na koribwata!” 
Ekero emeganda yamentekanete, agachaaka gokwana, “Ekegori eke nekegori ekebe, gekorigia egekone buna ekemanyererio, korende tigekoegwa egekone otatiga eki’omobani Yona. 
Naki buna Yona abeete ekemanyererio ase abanto ba Ninawi, naboigo Omwana‐o‐Mwanyabaanto arache koba ase ekegori eke. 
Enting’ana ya Irianyi netenene ase rituko ri’ekiina amo nabaanto b’ekegori eke; ere nabanachere ekiina, ekiagera nigo arwete ase chinsinyo chi’ense anyare koigwa obong’aini bwa Sulemani; na rora, oyobuete Sulemani naiga are. 
Abanto ba Ninawi nabatenene ase rituko ri’ekiina amo nabaanto b’ekegori eke, na barabwo nabakenachere ekiina ekegori eke, ekiagera baonchogete ase okorandia kwa Yona; na rora, oyobuete Yona naiga are. 
“Omonto gatongirie etaaya tari koyebeeka ase ekenunchi gose ayemumeke negetonga, korende nigo akoyebeeka igoro ase ekebekerero, erinde abagosoa barore oborabu. 
Etaaya y’omobere oo neriiso riao; ekero eriso riao rire rigiya, omobere oo bwonsi nigo oichire noborabu; korende ekero eriiso riao rire riibe, omobere oo bwonsi nigo oichire nomosunte. 
Gaki renda, oborabu obwo bore ime yao tibocha koba omosunte. 
Nabo omobere oo bwonsi korabe noborabu, ensemo ende teba omosunte, omobere oo noichore noborabu kegima, buna etaya ase amasase aye egwoka oborabu.” 
Ekero arenge gokwana, Omofarisai oyomo akamorangeria aragere amo nere. Agasoa, agaikaransa koragera. 
Omofarisai oria agachia korora, agakumia ekiagera ataisabete ritang’ani atararagera. 
Omonene akamoteebia, “Bono inwe Abafarisai iga mogosibia egekombe na esani korwa isiko, korende ime yaino nigo moichire obouri na obobe. 
Inwe abariri, ere oyokorete egia isiko, tari ere okorete nekia ime nakerokio? 
Korende karue esadaka y’ebire ime; naende rora, ebinto bionsi nebichenu ase more. 
“Korende obobe nobwaino Abafarisai, ekiagera mokorua ensemo ya ikomi ye chiarare, na kera egesaku gie chinyeni, mogotiga oboronge na obwanchani bwa Nyasae. Naro aya nabagwenerete moyakore, motari gotiga ayande. 
Obobe nobwaino, Abafarisai! Ekiagera mwanchete goikaransa ebirogo bia bosio ase chisinagogi, na gokwanigwa namasikani ase chichiiro. 
Obobe nobwaino! Ekiagera mogwekaine chimbeera chitari kororekana, na abanto baeta igoro yaye batamanyeti.” 
Omworokia oyomo bw’amachiiko akairaneria, akamoteebia, “Omworokia, kogoteeba aya, nigo ogotochaaya naintwe.” 
Agateeba, “Nainwe aborokia b’amachiiko, obobe nobwaino! Ekiagera mogotweka abanto chigurube chindito, na inwe abanyene timori gokuna chigurube echio nonya nase ekemo ki’ebiara biaino. 
Obobe nobwaino! Ekiagera mokoagacha chimbeera chi’ababani, abwo chisokoro chiaino chiaitete. 
Naboigo inwe mwabeire abarori na gwanchera ogokora gwe chisokoro chiaino, ekiagera nabwo baabaitete, na inwe mokoagacha chimbeera chiabo. 
Nase ayio obong’aini bwa Nyasae nigo bwateebete: Nintome ase bare ababani na abatomwa; baite abande babo na kobachanda, 
erinde amanyinga y’ababani bonsi, ayaitegete korwa omochakano bw’ense naganigwe korwa ase ekegori eke, 
korwa amanyinga y’Abeli goika amanyinga a Zakaria; oria oiteretwe gati y’egesasiimero ne Hekalu. Ee, nabateebirie, narigigwe korwa ase ekegori eke. 
Obobe nobwaino, inwe aborokia b’amachiiko! Ekiagera mwarusirie ekeigorero gie chisemi; inwe abanyene timwasoa, na baria abaare gosoa mwabatangire.” 
Agachia gosoka korwa aroro, aborokia b’amariiko na Abafarisai bagachaaka komomiga namaborio amakong’u, na komochegia akwane igoro y’ebinto ebinge; 
bakomotega, banyore ring’ana korwa ase omonwa oye, erinde banyare komosoera.
